Privacy Policy

ReelCoach — Built by Amazing Apps
Last updated: November 2026

ReelCoach is a browser extension that adds playback controls to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok videos. It is designed to respect your privacy and operates without collecting personal information.

Key Summary: ReelCoach operates 100% locally in your browser. It makes zero external network requests, has no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and does not track your browsing activity.

What ReelCoach Collects

Nothing. ReelCoach has no servers, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no user accounts. Neither Amazing Apps nor any third party receives any data from your browser.

What is Stored on Your Device

ReelCoach stores only your personal playback preferences locally using Chrome's extension storage:

Setting Example Location
Skip duration (seconds) 5 Chrome sync storage
Shift + skip (seconds) 1 Chrome sync storage
Hide controls delay 2.5 Chrome sync storage
Mirror mode on/off false Chrome sync storage
Extension power state true Chrome sync storage

If you have Chrome Sync enabled, Google Chrome syncs these preferences between your devices. Removing the extension completely deletes this stored data.

Network Requests

ReelCoach makes zero network requests. It does not contact external APIs, does not download video files, and transmits no data whatsoever.

Permissions

  • storage: Used solely to remember your UI preferences (skip interval, hide delay, mirror state, and power toggle).

ReelCoach does not request download permissions, cookie access, identity permissions, or background network access.

Children's Privacy

ReelCoach does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
